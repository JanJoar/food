#+title: What Joar's Having
#+author: Joar von Arndt

This is project to document the food I eat, primarily during my visit
to the Republic of China (中華民國) during the autumn of 2026. This is
done both for your enjoyment and my benefit, as I realized during my
trip to the communist Chinese mainland that taking pictures of good
food you've had is a great inspiration for finding recipes that you
can later cook at home.

The project works as a sort of "sub-blog" to the main one at
[[https://joarvarndt.se][joarvarndt.se]], and uses the same [[https://codeberg.org/Joar/org-static-blog][org-static-blog]] tooling to generate
itself. Each image has its own page, containing an image and
optionally a small caption. The project homepage is automatically
generated by finding the names of each org-mode file, which will be
named after the day it covers.
