Japanese Beef Donburi and a couple of Onigiri.

Today I woke up very late (due to being jet-lagged) and ended up talking to my roommate the rest of the day. By the time I realized I had not eaten for 24 hours all the restaurants were closed, and so I went down to the local 7-eleven and bought some simple pre-prepared meals. The Donburi was very good, but the fillings of the Onigiri were disappointing for different reasons.
