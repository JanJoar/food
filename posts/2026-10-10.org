Conveyor belt sushi. Today is the Taiwanese national day; Double ten
(十十) day. It celebrates the Wǔchāng uprising (武昌起義) of the
[[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1911_Revolution][Xīnhài revolution]] of 1911 that toppled the Qing dynasty and led to the
creation of the Republic of China that is today Taiwan. Today is
therefore the [[https://joarvarndt.se/food/2026-09-21][115th year]] of the republic.

After waking up early to see a parade (it was not really visible to
the public) and airshow the party decided upon sushi, and we went to a
conveyor belt-sushi place in the Taipei main station.
