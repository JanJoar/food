Mango shaved ice with condensed milk. The [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango_shaved_ice][Wikipedia]] page for this dish "one of
the world's best desserts",[fn:1] although I can not find this claim repeated in
the sources cited. Nevertheless, it is not a controversial claim to make. The
simplicity of having condensed milk and ice — a sweet, simple, and yet filling
combination — works perfectly in the Taiwanese summer weather. In fact, the
climate is an integral ingredient to this dish; it would be impossible to
replicate this back in Stockholm. The mango (locally produced on Formosa and
immediately noticeable in its quality) compliments the clean flavour of the ice
wonderfully.
