This is the easiest and best value (if also most unhealthy) meal I
have made and eaten here. Homemade lǔ ròu fàn (滷肉饭), braised pork
rice, from a ready made packet for the price of only $60. Simply heat
the pork, boil some rice, and optionally chop some vegetables for a
filling meal.

It is probably a good thing that I can not get the critical ingredient
back home.
