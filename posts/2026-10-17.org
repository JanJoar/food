Aboriginal Taiwanese boar sausage. The Taiwanese identity is very
interesting in many ways; it includes the Austronesian people's that
have lived here for as long as humanity has, the old Chinese migrants
from the Ming and Qing dynasties, those from the Japanese colonial
period (although they are very few) and of course those that came with
the Kuomintang. All these parts synthesise into one identity, or part
of one. That is most obvious in the cuisine.
