Happy new year! Today is the final day of the 234th year of the French
revolutionary calendar (/Quintidi 5 jour complémentaire/). Tomorrow will
then be /Primidi 1 Vendémiaire an 235 de la Révolution/. To celebrate
this new year's eve I scouted out a French bakery and bought some
simple French croissants, as well as a baguette not pictured here.

Every single (European) foreigner I have met here has been of
unanimous opinion — the Taiwanese do not know how to make good bread.
The same is true of the mainlanders, although they add some foul
tasting additive to all the bread I had there [[https://joarvarndt.se/chinese_phenomenology.html][last year]]. But it seems
that it is still possible to find quality European-style baked goods
as long as one goes to a speciality location.
