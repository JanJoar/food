A humble bubble tea (珍珠奶茶). It is surprising that it has taken me
so long to highlight this, given that it is practically the national
drink of the country and that I had originally considered posting it
as the first picture upon arrival. They are quite filling for a simple
drink — the pearls are almost enough to fool you into thinking you've
had a proper meal.

Tonight I felt like taking a short stroll in the evening, and so
wandered up on the same mountain from [[https://joarvarndt.se/food/2026-10-03][a few days ago]], bringing
something with me to consume as I watched planes take off from
[[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songshan_Airport][Songshan]] behind the Taipei skyline.
