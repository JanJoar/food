A simple home made version of the Japanese Chazuke or
Taiwanese/Chinese Hakka Lei Cha. Rice with seaweed, peanuts, Alaska
pollock (crab sticks) seared in soy sauce, and some leafy greens all
in a bath of jasmine tea.
