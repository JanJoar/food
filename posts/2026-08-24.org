I went to the store today and, due to the constant heat, was craving
some watermelon. Imagine my surprise when instead of red, it was this
beautiful golden colour instead! Apparently this is a naturally
occurring version of watermelon, although this is my first time eating
it. Tastes quite similar to the red variety, if perhaps a bit sweeter.
