I remember having dragon fruit as a child and not liking it — not that
I actively disliked it, just that it did not taste as strongly as I
had hoped from its appearance (and name). Having it now I quite enjoy
it, and it is a good idea to take the opportunity to have tropical
fruits while I am here.
