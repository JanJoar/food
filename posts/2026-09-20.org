I was invited to go to a tea house with a friend and had some lovely
high-quality Pu'er tea while chatting along. Apparently the Pu'er tea
industry went through a large transformation in 2007, shifting
production from a high-quality artisanal industry towards large-scale
plantations that drove the quality down. Teas from before 2007 are
therefore often higher quality, something I found quite fascinating. I
also had this quite unique (to Asia) rice pudding alongside the tea,
which reminded me the Nordic /[[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risalamande#Risifrutti][Risifrutti]]/:
